# What it costs

Tend is free: it's open source, under the MIT licence. What costs something is somewhere to run it, and a domain.

| Where | Cost |
|---|---|
| Your computer | Nothing. Only you can see the site. |
| **Railway**, Hobby plan | **$5 a month, including $5 of usage, and a card.** A small site is meant to fit in what's included. Anything more is added to the bill. |
| A server of your own | About $5 to $7 a month for a small one |
| A domain | About $10 to $20 a year, from whoever you buy it |

Railway's price is from October 2026. [Its own page](https://railway.com/pricing) has today's.

## What Railway counts

Memory by the hour, processor time, the volume's size, and what's sent to visitors. Tend uses about 100 MB of memory when idle, and pages that were kept cost next to nothing to send. Photos are most of what's sent.

Tend's own blog is meant to run on Railway from the first release, and its monthly bill to be here, with its date.

## Set a limit

In Railway, open your workspace's **Usage**, then **Set usage limits**:

- A **custom email alert** writes to you at an amount you choose.
- A **hard limit** stops everything in the workspace at an amount you choose, your site too. Set it well above the alert.

## Keep it small

- Remove photos and files you no longer need from Media.
- Nothing else grows by itself. The copies Tend keeps never take more than a fifth of the disk.
