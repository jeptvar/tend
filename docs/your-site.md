# How your site looks

## Design

**Design** has your logo, site icon, accent colour, fonts, header, footer text and social links. On a wide window your site is beside them, as a computer or a phone shows it, and follows what you change. On any other, **Preview** shows it. Nothing changes for visitors until **Save design**, and after that there's an **Undo**.

The accent colour makes all the others: softer backgrounds, links, buttons and the bottom of the page. With **Clean** fonts, your site uses the ones on each visitor's device. **Classic** and **Friendly** come with Tend, and your site sends them itself.

If a colour is hard to read where links are, Tend says so and suggests one close to it.

Custom CSS is under **Advanced**, for people who know it. A mistake there can make your site hard to read, and it may stop working when the theme is updated.

A theme of your own shows there too: see [Themes](themes.md).

## The site menu

**Site menu** has the links at the top and the bottom of every page. Until you change it, the one at the top has every published page, and Blog once there are posts.

- **Add link** finds your pages, posts and tags, or takes an address you paste.
- The arrows change the order. **Put under the one above** puts a link beneath another, and **Move out** brings it back.
- **Save menu** puts it on your site, with an **Undo**.

A page that isn't on your site any more leaves the menu by itself, and comes back if it does.

## Business details

**Settings**, then **Business details**: your address, phone, email, opening hours and a link to a map. They show at the bottom of every page, in every Contact & hours section, and right under the Big intro on your homepage, and search engines are told. Your phone number is at the top of every page, too. Change them once, here.

## Who can see it

**Settings**, then **General**:

- **Everyone:** your site is open, and search engines can find it.
- **Coming soon:** visitors see a page with your name and details. You still see the real site while you're signed in.
- **Only people signed in:** visitors are asked to sign in, for pages and for pictures and files.

## Search and sharing

Titles, descriptions, the picture for sharing, the sitemap and the feed are filled in for you. **Settings**, then **Search & social** has the description of the site and the picture used when a page has none. Each post and page has its own under **Search and sharing** in its settings.

## Addresses

When you change the address of something that's published, the old one leads to the new one. To send other addresses somewhere, an old site's for example, add them in **Settings**, then **Search & social**.

## Code for every page

**Settings**, then **Advanced** takes code for the top or the end of every page, such as a visitor counter's. Pages are fast because they have no JavaScript. What you add there can change that, and runs for everyone who visits.
