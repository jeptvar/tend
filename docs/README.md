# The guide

Tend is an easy, fast CMS for a small website that you own. These pages say how to use it, and what to do when something isn't as it should be.

> Tend isn't released yet. These pages describe version 0.1 as it will be.

## Start here

- [Get started](get-started.md): on your computer, on Railway, or on a server of your own.
- [Where can Tend run?](where-can-tend-run.md)
- [Install Node.js](install-node.md), which Tend needs on your computer.
- [What it costs](costs.md)

## Using your site

- [Write and publish](writing.md): posts, pages, photos, the Trash and earlier versions.
- [How your site looks](your-site.md): design, the site menu, business details, and who can see it.

## When something goes wrong

- [Error messages](errors/README.md), one page for each.
- In the admin, **Help** has short answers, and says how Tend is doing.
- `tendcms doctor` checks what Tend needs to run where it is, and says how to fix what's wrong.

## About these pages

Each is at `https://tendcms.dev/docs/` followed by its name, such as `https://tendcms.dev/docs/backups`, and an error's at `https://tendcms.dev/errors/` followed by its code. Short addresses lead to some of them:

| Address | Page |
|---|---|
| `https://tendcms.dev/install-node` | [Install Node.js](install-node.md) |
| `https://tendcms.dev/online` | [Move your site online](move-your-site.md) |
