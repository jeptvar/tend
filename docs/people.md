# People

Everyone who can sign in to your site is under **People**, which Admins see.

| | Editor | Admin | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Write and publish posts and pages | yes | yes | yes |
| Add, describe and replace photos and files | yes | yes | yes |
| Move things to the Trash, and restore them | yes | yes | yes |
| Site menu | yes | yes | yes |
| Design, Settings and People | | yes | yes |
| Delete things forever | | yes | yes |
| Invite and change Editors | | yes | yes |
| Invite and change Admins | | | yes |
| Download backups | | | yes |
| Hand the site over | | | yes |

There's one **Owner**: whoever set the site up, until they hand it over.

## Invite someone

1. Choose **People**.
2. Type their email, and choose what they can do.
3. Choose **Make an invite link**, then **Copy link**, and send it to them however suits. Tend sends no email.

The link works once, for 7 days. They choose a name and a password, and get a recovery kit of their own. **Take back** ends a link that hasn't been used. A new invite for the same email ends the old one.

## Change what someone can do

Choose another role beside their name, then **Change**. They're signed out, and have it when they sign in again.

**Stop them signing in** keeps someone out for now, and signs them out everywhere. **Let them sign in again** takes it back. **Remove** takes their account away. What they wrote stays on the site either way.

## When someone forgets their password

Beside their name, choose **Make a new-password link** and send it to them. It works once, for a day. They can also use a code from their recovery kit: see [Locked out](locked-out.md).

## Hand the site over

Beside the person who'll have it, who has to be an Admin, choose **Make them the Owner**, and type your password. You stay as an Admin. Only they can hand it back.
