# Email and password don't match

> That email and password don't match.

## What happened

Tend says the same whether the email has no account here, the password is wrong, or the account is kept from signing in, so that nobody can find out who has an account.

## What to do

- Check the email for a typing mistake, and that Caps Lock is off.
- Use **Show password** to see what you typed.
- Choose **Forgot password?** A code from your recovery kit lets you choose a new password straight away.
