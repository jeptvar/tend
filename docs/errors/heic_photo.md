# HEIC photos

> That's a HEIC photo, the kind iPhones take, and Tend can't use those yet.

## What happened

HEIC needs a licensed decoder, which Tend doesn't come with.

## What to do

- On the iPhone: Settings, Camera, Formats, **Most Compatible**. Photos you take from then on are JPEGs.
- For one you have: on a Mac, open it in Preview, choose File, Export, and pick JPEG. On the iPhone, share it by email to yourself, which makes it a JPEG.
